Personas

Swap between specialist AIs without restarting. Ships with nova-default, dev-sentinel, researcher, and automator. Each has its own system prompt, model, and skill set. Drop a SPIRIT.md file into ~/.nova/personas/ to add your own.

Skills

Questions people ask

The fine print,
in plain English.

Why is Nova cheaper than OpenClaw? +

OpenClaw's monthly fee covers three consumables: the server their agent runs on, the messaging infrastructure, and bulk LLM tokens. Nova pushes all three onto assets you already own — your computer, your own Telegram bot, your own Anthropic key. That's why Nova can be a one-time buy and OpenClaw can't.

Do I need a server or a domain? +

No. Nova runs on whatever computer you already use. Cloudflare Tunnel gives you a free *.trycloudflare.com HTTPS URL with no account, no domain, no port forwarding. The onboard wizard sets all of this up for you automatically.

What if my computer sleeps? +

Nova can't reply if the machine is off. The onboard wizard configures sleep prevention while Nova is running. For 24/7 availability, install Nova on a spare machine, a Mac mini, or a $35 Raspberry Pi — it runs on 3 watts.

Is my data on your servers? +

No. Nothing touches Elevare servers. Nova stores its config, memory, logs, and skills under ~/.nova/ on your machine. Messages go directly from Telegram → your Cloudflare Tunnel → your gateway → Anthropic → back to you. Zero middleware, zero telemetry.

Can I write my own skills and personas? +

Yes — and it's the whole point. A skill is a folder with SKILL.md and tools.py. A persona is a SPIRIT.md with a system prompt body. Both hot-reload. Full spec in ARCHITECTURE.md.

What about support? +

Spark: GitHub issues. Home and above: email. Pro and above: priority email. Lifetime: priority plus one hour of custom persona tuning from the Elevare studio team.
