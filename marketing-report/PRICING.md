## Recommended Elevare tiers

Elevare sits in the "small agency / premium freelancer" bracket — higher quality than a Fiverr gig, more personal than a mid-size agency. Price on **value and specificity**, not hours.

- **Target:** clients who finished Tier 2 or 3 and want ongoing execution

## Pricing rules of thumb

1. **Never quote hourly.** Value-based pricing only. A report that unlocks $50k of revenue is worth $5k regardless of hours.
2. **Anchor high.** Lead with Tier 3, then present Tier 2 as the "most popular" option. Most clients will pick Tier 2.
3. **Deposit:** 50% up front, 50% on delivery. No exceptions for new clients.
4. **Scope creep:** revisions beyond the included rounds are $150/hr or a flat $500 per extra revision cycle.
5. **Rush fee:** +50% for delivery under 7 days.
6. **Niche premium:** once Elevare builds 3+ reports in the same vertical (e.g. hair salons), raise prices 25% for that niche and lead with case studies.
