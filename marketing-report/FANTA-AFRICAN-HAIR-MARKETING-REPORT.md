## 4. Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Pawtucket/Providence Area)

The African hair braiding market in Pawtucket/Providence has several established salons. Key competitive factors:

| Factor | Fanta's Position |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Rating** | 4.7 stars - strong |
| **Hours** | 7 days/week - best availability |
| **Walk-ins** | Accepted - competitive advantage |
| **Online presence** | Website by ElevareStudio - professional |
| **Social media** | Opportunity for growth |
| **Pricing** | Competitive with market ($40-$200+) |

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Open 7 days a week.** Many competitors close on Mondays or Sundays. Fanta captures clients who need weekend or last-minute availability.
2. **Walk-ins accepted.** Reduces friction for first-time clients who don't want to commit without seeing the salon.
3. **$50 deposit (not full prepay).** Low commitment to book, high enough to reduce no-shows.
4. **Professional website.** Most braiding salons rely only on Instagram or Facebook. Having a dedicated site builds trust.
5. **4.7-star rating.** Strong social proof, but needs more reviews to dominate local search.

### Weaknesses to Address

1. **37 reviews is too few.** Competitors with 100+ reviews rank higher in Google Maps. Every satisfied client should be asked to leave a review.
2. **Social media presence needs growth.** Transformation content (before/after) is the #1 growth driver for braiding salons on Instagram and TikTok.
3. **No visible referral program.** Braiding clients talk to friends. Formalize it.

### Voice Analysis (from website copy and business positioning)

| Attribute | Fanta's Voice |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Tone** | Warm, confident, welcoming |
| **Register** | Professional but approachable |
| **Audience** | Women who want quality protective styles without pretension |
| **Key message** | Expert braiding, walk-in friendly, open every day |
| **What to avoid** | Corporate stiffness, over-promising, fake luxury language |

### Voice Guidelines for All Content

**Do:**
- Use "you" and "your" (talk to the client, not about her)
- Show the work (photos, videos, results)
- Be direct about pricing and availability
- Keep captions short on Instagram, conversational on TikTok
- Let client reactions and testimonials speak

**Don't:**
- Use "In today's rapidly evolving beauty landscape" (AI slop)
- Say "game-changer" or "revolutionary"
- Over-explain. A great braid speaks for itself.
- Use corporate language. This is a neighborhood salon, not a chain.
- Fake casualness. Be real.

### Sample Voice (Before/After)

**Before (AI slop):**
> "At Fanta African Hair Braiding, we are committed to delivering exceptional protective styling services that combine traditional African braiding techniques with modern innovation, ensuring every client leaves feeling beautiful, empowered, and confident."

## 6. Content Strategy

*Built using the content-engine skill: platform-native content, source-first, no engagement bait.*

### Content Pillars (4 rotating themes)

| Pillar | What it is | Why it works | Post frequency |
|--------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| **The Work** | Before/after transformations, close-up shots of finished styles | Visual proof. This is what makes people book. | 3x/week |
| **Style Education** | "Knotless vs. box braids: what's the difference?" explainers | Answers the questions people Google before booking. | 1x/week |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Braiding process videos, day-in-the-life, salon vibes | Builds trust. Clients want to see the space before they come. | 1x/week |
| **Client Love** | Reviews, testimonials, client selfies after their appointment | Social proof. Nothing sells like a happy client showing off. | 1x/week |

### Platform-Specific Rules

#### Instagram
- **Feed:** High-quality transformation photos (before/after side-by-side)
- **Reels:** Braiding process timelapses (30-60 seconds), style reveals
- **Stories:** Daily "available today" updates, poll/quiz engagement, client selfies
- **Captions:** 1-2 sentences max. Let the photo do the work. Include price + style name.

#### TikTok
- **Format:** Short transformation reveals (15-30 seconds), trending audio
- **Hook:** First 2 seconds must show the "after" or a dramatic moment
- **Hashtags:** #knotlessbraids #boxbraids #africanbraiding #protectivestyles #braidingpawtucket #providenceri
- **Frequency:** 3-5x/week minimum. TikTok rewards consistency.

### Content Production Workflow

1. **Every client = content.** Ask permission to photograph/video the finished style.
2. **Before photo first.** Always take a before shot. The transformation is what people share.
3. **Natural lighting.** Stand the client near the window. Phone camera is fine.
4. **Post same day.** Fresh content performs better than backlogged content.
5. **Tag the client.** They'll reshare it. Free distribution.

## 8. Recommended Campaigns

### Campaign 1: "100 Reviews" Push

**Goal:** Get from 37 to 100+ Google reviews in 90 days.

**How:**
- Print a small sign at checkout: "Loved your braids? Leave us a Google review!"
- After every appointment, send a text/DM: "Thanks for coming in! If you loved your style, we'd appreciate a quick Google review: [link]"
- Offer a small incentive: "$10 off your next appointment when you leave a Google review"
- Track weekly. 37 → 100 in 90 days = ~7 reviews/week

**Why it matters:** Google Maps rankings are heavily influenced by review count + rating. Moving from 37 to 100+ reviews can jump you from page 2 to the top 3 results for "hair braiding near me."

### Campaign 2: "Refer a Friend" Program

**Goal:** Turn every satisfied client into a growth channel.

**How:**
- "Refer a friend, you both get $15 off your next appointment"
- Simple tracking: client gives friend's name at booking
- Promote on Instagram Stories and in-salon signage

**Expected result:** If 20% of clients refer one person per quarter, that's steady organic growth with zero ad spend.

### Campaign 3: "First-Time Client" TikTok Series

**Goal:** Build TikTok following and attract new clients through transformation content.

**How:**
- Film every first-time client's transformation (with permission)
- Use the format: "She walked in wanting [style]. Here's what she left with."
- Post 3-5x/week with relevant hashtags
- Include location tag (Pawtucket, RI) in every video

**Why TikTok:** Hair braiding content regularly goes viral on TikTok. A single transformation video can reach 100K+ views and drive bookings for weeks.
