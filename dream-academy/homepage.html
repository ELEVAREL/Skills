FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you enroll.

How long does it take to get my diploma?

Most students complete the program in 6–18 months, depending on how many credits you already have. We'll assess your transcripts and give you a clear timeline during your first visit.

Is this a GED? What's the difference?

No — Dream Academy awards an actual accredited high school diploma, not a GED certificate. A diploma is more widely recognized by employers and colleges.

What are the class hours?

We offer morning and afternoon sessions, Monday through Friday, 9 AM – 4 PM. We work with your schedule to find the best fit.

How much does it cost?

Contact us at (804) 228-8200 for current tuition information. We work to make education accessible and can discuss options during your assessment.

Do I need my old transcripts?

They help, but they're not required to get started. We can often retrieve your records or assess your level through placement testing.

Can I attend if I have a job?

Absolutely. Many of our students work full-time. We offer flexible scheduling and hybrid options to fit around your work life.
