Executive Summary

The Bottom Line

Dream Academy's current website is actively hurting enrollment. Here's what we found and why it matters.

We audited dreamacademyrichmond.com across five categories: design & branding, user experience, content strategy, mobile & performance, and SEO. The site scored 31 out of 100 — a failing grade driven by four critical issues, four major issues, and two minor ones.

The root cause is clear: the site is a generic Wix template with zero customization, and several core elements are broken or misconfigured (swapped navigation links, a stock-photo flower in place of a phone icon). Social proof exists but is buried below the fold. There's no hero hook, no dominant call-to-action, and "Richmond VA" appears 14 times per page — which reads as SEO spam rather than genuine local presence.

The good news: every issue in this report is fixable with a complete rebuild and brand overhaul. The return is significant. Based on comparable adult-education sites we've worked on, a properly branded, conversion-focused homepage can double inquiry-to-enrollment rates within the first 90 days.

Critical Issues

Fix These First

Four issues are actively breaking the site or destroying trust. These must be addressed immediately.

Navigation links are swapped
Critical

"Elective Courses" and "Transcript Requests" point to each other's pages. Students clicking one land on the other.

Fix

Rebuild nav with correct routing. Add analytics to track click-throughs.

Phone icon is a stock photo of a purple flower
Critical

The "call us" icon in the header is a stock image of a flower — not a phone. It's the first thing visitors see and it signals complete carelessness.

Fix

Replace with a proper phone icon. Add click-to-call on mobile.

Generic Wix template, zero brand identity
Critical

The site uses a stock Wix template with default colors, fonts, and layout. Nothing distinguishes Dream Academy from any other generic business site.

Fix

Build a custom brand system (colors, typography, visual elements) and apply consistently.

No hero hook, no dominant CTA, form competing with headline
Critical

The hero section has no compelling message and no clear primary action. A contact form sits next to the headline, fighting for the same attention. Visitors don't know what to do.

Fix

Redesign hero with a single dominant CTA ("Call Now" or "Enroll Today"). Move the form to a dedicated section.

Major & Minor Issues

Everything Else

Four major issues degrade conversion. Two minor ones hurt SEO. All are fixable in the rebuild.

Social proof buried below the fold
Major

Testimonials and success stories exist but require significant scrolling. Visitors leave before seeing them.

Fix

Surface graduate testimonials immediately after the hero section.

"Richmond VA" repeated 14 times per page
Major

Keyword stuffing from an old-school SEO playbook. Reads as spam to both visitors and modern search engines.

Fix

Rewrite copy naturally. Use schema markup and local business structured data instead.

No program comparison or pricing transparency
Major

Visitors can't see what makes Dream Academy different from a GED program or online diploma mill. No pricing information anywhere.

Fix

Add a comparison table (Dream Academy vs GED vs diploma mills) and publish transparent pricing.

No graduation stats or credibility metrics
Major

Zero numbers on the site. No "500+ graduates," no "93% completion rate," no years in operation. Trust signals missing.

Fix

Add a stat bar with real numbers directly under the hero section.

Thin duplicate location pages
Minor

Multiple pages targeting different Richmond neighborhoods with near-identical content. Google penalizes this.

Fix

Consolidate into one location page with proper local schema.

No director video or personal introduction
Minor

Adult learners are making a big commitment. A 90-second video from the director would dramatically increase trust.

Fix

Record a simple welcome video from the director. Embed on the About section.
